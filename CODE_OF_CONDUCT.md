# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to keep this project a place where people are treated with respect.
Discussions here touch passport numbers and identity documents, so bad faith has
real consequences for contributors and for the people whose data this software
touches.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith; disagree with the code, not the person.
- Never request, share, or paste real passport numbers, national IDs, captcha
  tokens, or session cookies — in issues, PRs, logs, or screenshots.
- Use synthetic fixtures in tests.
- Accept that CAPTCHA handling is human-only by design, and do not propose
  automation of it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, doxxing, sharing real identity documents, and any attempt to bypass
CAPTCHA or bot protection on external systems.

## Enforcement

Report privately to the maintainers. Reports are handled confidentially.
